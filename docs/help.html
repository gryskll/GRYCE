Getting into gryCE

gryCE, the GRYSKLL Creative Engine, is the crew's member portal. It's for GRYSKLL members right now. Once you're in, the full guide is under Help at the bottom of every page.

Signing in

  1. Open gryCE and tap Sign in with Google. Use the Google account you want to use for gryCE. It doesn't have to be a Gmail address, any Google account works.
  2. If you see "You're signed in, but not linked yet," text Shaah the email shown on that screen. Please don't post it in comments or group chats.
  3. Once Shaah adds it to your artist profile, your page fills in within about 30 minutes. Refresh and you're in.

If it's not working

  • Signed in with the wrong Google account? Tap Sign out at the top right, then sign in with the right one.
  • Still says not linked after Shaah added you? Give it 30 minutes, then refresh. If it still says not linked, text Shaah a screenshot.
  • The Google screen won't open or keeps looping? Open gryCE in your regular browser (Safari or Chrome), not inside Instagram, Patreon or another app.
