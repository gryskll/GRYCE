# gryCE changelog

What changed in each release, in the words members see. Newest first.

## v0.4.14, November 20, 2026
- Funding: under All stages, each stage is its own colored box. Done stages are green, the open stage amber, and stages not started yet gray.
- Projects: a project ticked Leadership Only in Notion shows only to Leadership while it's being sorted out. Leadership sees a Leadership only tag on it.
- Projects: On Hold gets its own group.

## v0.4.13, November 20, 2026
- Project updates is now **Projects**, for everyone: the active project at the top with its full card, then every other GRYSKLL project grouped by status, so the crew can see what's in the pipeline.
- What you owe has section cards across the top. Tap one to jump to that part.
- Crew news, Crew tasks, In the works and Discography are grouped in colored sections: news by new and read, tasks by where they stand, songs by stage, releases by year.
- Leadership opens on section cards for Documents and Contributions, each with its own page.

## v0.4.12, November 20, 2026
- The footer credit reads "Designed BetweenKeys", with BetweenKeys linking to betweenkeys.org.

## v0.4.11, November 19, 2026
- Help explains the colored groups, the document registry by status, and how contributions are tied to people.

## v0.4.10, November 19, 2026
- Colored groups across gryCE, so you can tell sections apart at a glance. Red is owed or holding a project up, amber is waiting on you, orange wants your input, blue is tasks, songs and drafts, green is money raised or approved.
- Leadership's document registry is grouped by status.

## v0.4.9, November 19, 2026
- Leadership: every contribution names who gave it, a crew member or a supporter. Supporters' contact details never show in gryCE.

## v0.4.8, November 19, 2026
- Leadership: contribution groups stand out more. Money that came in before a campaign started shows first, under Before the album.

## v0.4.7, November 19, 2026
- Leadership: contributions grouped by project, then funding stage, then contributor type.

## v0.4.6, November 19, 2026
- "Developed by BetweenKeys" in the footer.

## v0.4.5, November 19, 2026
- The sign-in page spells out GRYSKLL Creative Engine, cycling through Community Engine and Collaborative Engine.

## v0.4.4, November 19, 2026
- Leadership: documents that need everyone's input show up as Input needed.

## v0.4.3, November 19, 2026
- Leadership: documents waiting on review show under What you owe.
- Home greets you with Peace.

## v0.4.2, November 19, 2026
- How gryCE works is a button at the top of Home.

## v0.4.1, November 19, 2026
- ISRC codes: ISRC Admins can issue the next code to a song from gryCE. gryCE picks the number, so a code can never be issued twice.
- Leadership section: documents waiting on Leadership, the document registry, and every contribution toward a project.

## v0.4, November 19, 2026
- Funding opens on the current stage, with buttons for Current stage and All stages.
- New song stage: Awaiting Mix Funding, for songs ready to mix and waiting on the money for it.

## v0.3.7, November 19, 2026

- Green skull and a new sign-in line: Don't let it die on your hard drive.

## v0.3.6, November 19, 2026

## v0.3.5, November 19, 2026

## v0.3.4, November 19, 2026

## v0.3.3, November 19, 2026
